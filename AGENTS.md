# Sapphire Nexus Agent Charter

Sapphire Nexus is Ari's clean next-generation intelligence kernel. It exists to
replace scattered, overgrown Sapphire-era experiments with one small,
source-rights-aware, local-first system.

## Mission

- Turn Ari's AI, quant, trading-research, repo, and runtime context into
  useful intelligence products.
- Preserve purpose-built apps such as THO / Project-Go-Forward, 0guard,
  wildfire-watch, and hackathon submissions as separate products.
- Prefer contracts and adapters over copying old implementation debt.
- Keep risky domains paper-only, dry-run, or read-only until a reversible
  approval plan exists.

## Hard Stops

- Do not touch THO, Project-Go-Forward, or TexasHomeOutlet assets from this repo.
- Do not execute live trades, payment settlement, wallet signing, or money
  movement.
- Do not send Telegram, social, email, or customer messages.
- Do not expose, copy, rotate, or embed secrets.
- Do not delete source repos, production data, DNS, GCP projects, Firestore,
  GCS, or runtime assets.
- Do not broaden repository, cloud, machine, or account permissions without an
  explicit prepared plan.

## Build Rules

- Use small typed contracts and tests before adding adapters.
- Store derived metadata, links, hashes, summaries, and provenance, not raw
  private payloads.
- Treat market outputs as research context, never investment advice or execution.
- Treat local model and Windows GPU access as gateway contracts first.
- Keep the first screen operational and data-backed, not a landing page.
